# Academic Paper Review

**Paper:** [Exact paper title]

**Authors / venue / year:** [Bibliographic information supported by the input]

**Paper version / source:** [Version, DOI or link when known]

> **Template guidance (do not reproduce):** Produce cohesive scholarly prose, not a question-by-question worksheet. Default target: **1,000–1,200 English words across the six sections**, adjustable to the paper's evidentiary complexity and the user's explicit requirements. Keep all six sections; protect decisive findings, original-paper citations, methodological context and material uncertainty even when compressing. Preserve material original-paper citations and uncertainties; avoid unsupported claims. The six sections below are the final output format for Skill 3 and are intentionally different from Skill 1/2's eight-step detailed report.

## 1. Summary

[One compact paragraph describing the research problem, motivation, core approach and most important finding. Clearly attribute conclusions to authors when necessary.]

## 2. Background & Contributions

[Explain the specific gap in previous work, the claimed contributions, and their demonstrated novelty or limits of independent novelty verification. Avoid exhaustive prerequisite explanations.]

## 3. Methodology

[Explain the central design and its mechanisms, including decisive data, assumptions, evaluation procedure or theoretical argument. Identify the methodological choices most relevant to interpreting results. Cite the original paper where supported.]

## 4. Key Results & Evidence

[Select only the most informative results. Explain the evidence and what it supports or does not support, with source locations and necessary denominators, settings or qualifications. Do not reproduce every result table.]

## 5. Strengths & Limitations

[Provide 2–4 substantive, evidence-based analytical strengths and limitations in integrated paragraphs. Separate author-acknowledged limitations from independent concerns where important. Explain their consequence for validity, generalizability or utility. Do not repeat the full methodology or results.]

## 6. Overall Assessment

[Give a balanced, decisive judgment about contribution, confidence, implications and the most important open question or next investigation. Do not simply repeat the summary.]

**Source/verification limitations:** [One brief note only when necessary: inaccessible paper material, unverified references, or findings not independently checked.]
